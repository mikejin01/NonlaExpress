What Is Cà Phê Sữa Đá?

Cà phê sữa đá translates simply as "coffee, milk, ice." It starts with a phin, the small metal drip filter used all over Vietnam. Coffee drips slowly through the phin, which makes a brew that's much stronger and bolder than an ordinary drip coffee.

Traditionally, that coffee is stirred into sweetened condensed milk and poured over ice. The result is rich and sweet, with a deep, almost chocolatey bitterness underneath. Because it's so concentrated, it holds up to the ice instead of tasting watered down.
